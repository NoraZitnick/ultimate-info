September – November

Fall: the Developmental League

Fall is for learning and getting reps. BADA’s High School 5v5 Developmental League uses smaller 5-on-5 teams so every player gets more touches — it’s the best time for brand-new players to start. Teams are single-gender (Girls and Open divisions).

  • 5-on-5 · games to 11
  • 45-minute hard cap
  • ~$40 per player
  • Min. 10 players per team

Events & dates

  1. Mid-Sep → mid-Oct

    OAK Fall Mini League

    Six Sunday-evening sessions at Estuary Park: drills, small-sided “minis” and full-field scrimmages. All genders, grades 6–12, no tryout.

  2. Early Oct → early Nov

    5v5 League game days

    Five Sundays of games (2026: Oct 4, 11, 25, Nov 1 & 8). Mornings in Hayward (Bret Harte MS / Cannery Park), afternoons at Godfrey Park, Alameda.

  3. Early–mid Oct

    Winter club tryouts

    Gold Rush, Cali Roll, Storm and Frost hold tryouts once the fall season is underway. See the club page →

  4. Early–mid Nov

    League finale

    The last game day of the fall league (2026: Nov 8 in Hayward). Fall results don’t lock you onto the same spring team.

January – May

Spring: the single-gender league

Spring is the main high school season. Most school teams play BADA’s High School Spring League — full 7-on-7 under USA Ultimate rules — split into single-gender divisions:

Girls · FMP

For girls and female-matching players of any experience level.

Open D1 · MMP

The most competitive division (East & South Bay). Open to all genders; mostly boys.

Open D2 · MMP

The developmental, lower-pressure division (East Bay) for newer players.

  • 7-on-7 · USA Ultimate rules
  • Saturdays & Sundays
  • ~$57 Open / ~$40 Girls

Events & dates

  1. Early Jan → early Feb

    Registration

    Player and team registration opens in early January; teams must register by early February (2026: Jan 5 → Feb 8).

  2. Late Feb → late Apr

    Regular-season league weekends

    Teams play league games most weekends at fields around the Bay (2026: Feb 28 – Apr 26).

  3. Last weekend of Apr

    BADA Spring League Playoffs

    Bracket play to crown the Girls, Open D1 and Open D2 champions (2026: Apr 25–26).

  4. Mid-May

    California HS State Championships

    The best teams from across California meet for state titles in Girls D1, Open D1 and Open D2. 2026 was May 16–17 in Fresno.

Tryouts Jan – Feb · season June – August

Summer: club season (YCC)

Summer is all-star club ultimate. BUYA and OAK pick the best players from across the Bay to form age-group teams that train all summer for USA Ultimate’s Youth Club Championships (YCC) — the national championship for youth club teams.

  • U20 & U17 age groups
  • Girls · Boys/Open · Mixed
  • Tryout required

Events & dates

  1. Late Jan → Feb

    YCC tryouts

    Several tryout days around the Bay, including a joint BUYA/OAK tryout in SF (2026: Jan 25 – Feb 22).

  2. Apr → May

    Kickoff & light practices

    Team meeting and 1–2 practices a month while the spring school season finishes.

  3. Jun → Jul

    Summer training & scrimmages

    Practices jump to 2–3 times a week. Teams may add scrimmages or a warm-up tournament — each team sets its own schedule.

  4. Early Aug

    Youth Club Championships (YCC)

    Nationals! Divisions: U20 Boys, Girls & Mixed; U17 Boys & Girls; plus U17 Boys Division II. 2026 was in Aurora, CO — OAK’s smOAK won U20 Boys.

November – March

Bonus: winter high school club

Between fall and spring, four tryout-based club teams — Gold Rush and Cali Roll (BUYA), Storm and Frost (OAK) — practice a few weekends a month and travel to college tournaments like Santa Barbara Qualifiers (early Jan), Presidents’ Day Qualifiers (mid-Feb) and Emerald City Challenge in Seattle (late Feb / early Mar).
