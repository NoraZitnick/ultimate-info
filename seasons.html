September – November

Fall: Developmental League

Small-sided games with your school team. Best time to start if you’re new.

    1. Mid-Sep → mid-Oct

      OAK Fall Mini League

      Sunday-evening drills and scrimmages at Estuary Park. Grades 6–12, all genders, $150.

    2. Early Oct → early Nov

      5v5 League game days

      Five Sundays in Hayward and Alameda (2026: Oct 4 – Nov 8).

    November – early March

    Winter: Club Season

    Tryout Club teams that play college tournaments: Gold Rush and Cali Roll (BUYA), Storm and Frost (OAK).

      1. October

        Tryouts

        1–2 days per team. Register online first.

      2. Nov → early Mar

        Practices

        1–3 weekends a month.

      3. Early Jan

        Santa Barbara Qualifiers

        College tournament in Santa Barbara.

      4. February

        Pres Day Qualifiers / local events

        San Luis Obispo or a local tournament.

      5. Late Feb → early Mar

        Emerald City Challenge

        Season-ending trip to Seattle.

      January – May

      Spring: Single-Gender League

      The main high school season. School teams play in Girls (FMP) or Open (MMP). Open D1 is competitive; D2 is for newer players.

        1. Jan → early Feb

          Registration

          Teams register by early February.

        2. Late Feb → late Apr

          League weekends

          Games most weekends around the Bay.

        3. Late Apr

          Spring League Playoffs

          Finals for Girls, Open D1 and Open D2.

        4. Mid-May

          CA HS State Championships

          Top teams from across the state (2026 in Fresno).

        Tryouts January – February · season June – August

        Summer: Club Season (YCC)

        All-star Club teams from BUYA and OAK. The season ends at YCC, the youth national championship.

          1. Jan → Feb

            Tryouts

            Several days around the Bay, including a joint BUYA/OAK tryout.

          2. Apr → May

            Light practices

            1–2 a month while spring wraps up.

          3. Jun → Jul

            Summer training

            Practices 2–3 times a week.

          4. Early Aug

            Youth Club Championships

            Nationals (2026 in Aurora, CO).
