Who We Are

What is BADA GURLS?

BADA GURLS is a program built to help grow the next generation of women in ultimate frisbee. It stands for Gender in Ultimate Revolution Leadership Series, and is a youth leadership initiative run by the Bay Area Disc Association (BADA) to develop young female leaders in the sport of Ultimate Frisbee.

Now in our third year, GURLS is a program within BADA focused on making ultimate frisbee more accessible, welcoming, and easier to learn for new players. We recognize that getting started in frisbee can sometimes feel overwhelming. New players often have the passion and excitement to improve but aren’t always sure where to start, what skills to work on, or where to find the resources to help them grow.

That’s where we come in!

Our goal is to bridge that gap by giving new players the tools, knowledge, and resources they need to develop their skills and confidence. Through guidance, skill-building resources, and a supportive community, we want to make the learning process less intimidating and help players understand what it takes to get better.

Our Team

We are a group of passionate ultimate frisbee players who want to make the sport more accessible to everyone. As we enter our third year, we are continuing to build on the GURLS program and find new ways to support players at every stage of their frisbee journey.

We believe that everyone deserves the opportunity to learn, improve, and feel confident on the field. Whether you’re picking up a frisbee for the first time or looking to take your game to the next level, GURLS is here to help you find your place in the sport.

Our Mission

To help youth ultimate players gain more knowledge about the Bay Area scene so they feel more confident and provide access to resources to help them become better players.
