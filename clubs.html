Club Teams

Club teams

Tryout teams that pull players from across the Bay. Winter teams play college tournaments; summer teams play at nationals.

How it works

  1. 1

    Play for your school

    Fall and spring leagues with your school or local team.

  2. 2

    Register & try out

    Winter tryouts in October, summer in January–February. Small tryout fee.

  3. 3

    Make the roster

    19–26 players. You’ll need a USA Ultimate membership and a season fee ($325 for BUYA winter).

  4. 4

    Practice & travel

    A few weekends a month in winter, 2–3 times a week in summer, plus travel.

💸 Both programs offer financial aid: BUYA scholarships ↗ · OAK financial aid ↗
