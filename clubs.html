Club Teams

How club ultimate works

Your school team is where you start. Club teams are tryout-based teams that pull the best players from all over the Bay to play at a higher level — in the winter against college teams and in the summer at nationals.

The path, step by step

  1. 1

    Play for your school

    Join your school or local team in the fall and spring leagues. Club coaches scout these games.

  2. 2

    Register & try out

    Sign up online and pay a small tryout fee. Winter tryouts are in October; summer (YCC) tryouts are in January–February.

  3. 3

    Make the roster

    Rosters are about 19–26 players. You’ll need a USA Ultimate membership and a season fee (e.g. $325 for BUYA winter teams).

  4. 4

    Practice & travel

    Practices are a few weekends a month in winter and 2–3 times a week in summer, plus travel tournaments.

💸 Money shouldn’t stop you: both programs offer financial aid — BUYA scholarships ↗ · OAK financial aid ↗

BUYA

Bay Ultimate Youth

Founded in 2020 to bring the Bay Area’s winter club teams (Gold Rush & Cali Roll) and its summer YCC teams under one USA Ultimate affiliate. BUYA’s goal is to help players reach excellence on the field while growing as leaders and community builders.

  • Website ↗
  • club@bayultimateyouth.org (winter)
  • ycc@bayultimateyouth.org (summer)
OAK

Oakland Ultimate

An Oakland-based youth program with a player-centered, team-focused approach. OAK runs development programs (Fall Mini League, free-agent teams) and competitive club teams in winter and summer — including smOAK, the 2025 & 2026 U20 Boys national champions.
